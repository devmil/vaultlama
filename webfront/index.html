VaultLama is being built as an open-source password manager client for your own Vaultwarden server. It is planned for phones, tablets, desktops, the web and browsers, with autofill where you sign in.

01 / Overview

A client for the server you choose.

Vaultwarden is a community-maintained server that works with Bitwarden clients. VaultLama is meant to be the app on your devices that connects to it, with no VaultLama account and no VaultLama cloud.

Your Vaultwarden server

You enter your server’s address and see which server receives your sign-in. Your encrypted vault lives there and on your devices.

Open source

VaultLama is meant to be inspected, changed and shared. The exact open-source license is chosen once the code it reuses has been reviewed.

On all your devices

One shared security core in Rust, with apps for phones, tablets and desktops, a web vault and browser extensions.

VaultLama is an independent project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Bitwarden, Inc. or the Vaultwarden project. Bitwarden is a trademark of Bitwarden, Inc.

02 / Principles

Security decides when it is ready.

A polished screen or a working sign-in does not make a password manager trustworthy. These principles from the project’s vision decide what ships, and when.

  1. 01

    Security determines readiness

    A feature ships only after its trust boundaries and failure modes have been assessed.

  2. 02

    Established cryptography

    VaultLama reuses reviewed implementations and tests compatibility with Vaultwarden. It never invents its own encryption.

  3. 03

    Minimal secret exposure

    Keys stay in the security core. A decrypted field appears only for the task that needs it, and is masked again afterwards.

  4. 04

    Works offline

    Your downloaded vault stays encrypted on the device and opens after you unlock it, even when the server is out of reach.

  5. 05

    Never silently lose data

    Conflicts, interrupted saves, unknown fields and server errors end in a state you can recover from.

  6. 06

    You stay in control

    Your vault never depends on a VaultLama account or on a service that sees your passwords. Locked, offline or syncing: the state is always clear.

Before it holds real passwords

A release is recommended for real credentials only after this evidence exists for that release:

  • Interoperability tests with supported Vaultwarden versions and another client.
  • Adversarial tests with malformed data, mixed-up accounts, lock races and lookalike websites.
  • Recovery tests for crashes, interrupted writes and conflicting edits.
  • Tests of the installed apps on real devices, including autofill.
  • An independent security assessment, with its scope and remaining limits published.
  • Signed releases and updates, and a private way to report vulnerabilities.

None of this exists yet. VaultLama has not had a security audit.

03 / Planned

What VaultLama is planned to do.

The goal is an everyday password manager for individuals, families and small teams. None of it is available yet.

Autofill everywhere Planned

Fill sign-ins in browsers and mobile apps after VaultLama has checked the website or app that asks. Nothing is filled without a deliberate tap or click.

Offline vault Planned

Search and read your vault without a connection. Changes made offline stay encrypted on the device until they can sync.

Sharing Planned

Share logins with family and small teams through Vaultwarden organizations and collections, with clear ownership and permissions.

Passkeys Planned

Store and use passkeys for websites and apps through the system’s own interfaces, where Vaultwarden and the system support them.

Generator Planned

Strong passwords, passphrases and usernames, created on your device. Save a new login, or update one after a password change.

Security reports Planned

Find weak and reused passwords, so you know which ones to change first.

Import and export Planned

Bring your vault from another password manager, export an encrypted backup, and check that it can be restored.

Lock and unlock Planned

Unlock with your master password or an enrolled system method such as a fingerprint, and lock again on your terms.

Two-step login Planned

Sign in with the second factors your Vaultwarden server supports. Certificate errors never come with a shortcut around the check.

05 / Privacy

Your vault goes to your server. Nowhere else.

VaultLama is designed so that the only service it talks to about your vault is the Vaultwarden server you chose.

  • No VaultLama account and no VaultLama cloud. Nothing about your vault goes to Devmil Solutions.
  • No analytics, telemetry or advertising in the app, and no secrets in logs or crash reports.
  • Today’s development builds make no internet connections at all.
  • This website has no analytics and no cookies, and loads nothing from other servers.

06 / Status

Development status

VaultLama is in its foundation phase: the app shell, the Rust core and the build pipeline exist, but no vault features yet. There are no releases. Please do not use development builds with real credentials.

No public release yet. Downloads appear here once one is published.

Milestones, ordered by dependency, not by date

  1. 1Next

    Prove the risky boundaries

    Core reuse, protocol behaviour, browser origin matching and mobile autofill, tested with made-up credentials on disposable servers.

  2. 2Later

    Shared vault foundation

    Sign-in, encrypted cache, search, reveal and locking.

  3. 3Later

    Editing and autofill

    Durable encrypted changes, sync between two clients, backup and restore, the first autofill.

  4. 4Later

    Every platform

    The proven core on every desktop, mobile, web and browser surface.

  5. 5Later

    Earn trust

    Independent assessment, signed releases and updates, and an ongoing response to reported vulnerabilities.

07 / Transparency
