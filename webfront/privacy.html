Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This policy explains how the VaultLama website at devmil.de/vaultlama and the VaultLama app handle personal data. VaultLama is a password manager client for Vaultwarden servers and is in early development.

Michael Lamers

Email: info [at] devmil [dot] de

2. Overview

This website and VaultLama are built to collect as little as possible. Devmil Solutions does not run analytics, advertising or tracking. The website sets no cookies and loads nothing from third-party servers: its fonts, images and scripts are served with the site itself. VaultLama has no VaultLama account and no VaultLama cloud service. Your vault is meant to live on the Vaultwarden server you choose and, encrypted, on your devices; Devmil Solutions does not receive it.

3. Hosting

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When the site is visited, GitHub processes technical access data to deliver it and keep it secure: IP address, date and time, requested page, referrer, browser and operating system. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a secure and reliable website).

GitHub may process data in the USA. GitHub, Inc. is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (adequacy decision, Art. 45 GDPR). Details: GitHub privacy statement. Devmil Solutions has no access to these logs.

4. Downloads and release data

The download list on the home page and the release notes are read from a file on this website. There is no public release yet. Once releases are published, the download links point to GitHub Releases, and the files are delivered by GitHub from github.com and its download servers. When you download a file, GitHub processes the same technical access data as described under Hosting, on the same legal basis and under the same privacy statement.

5. Local storage in your browser

When you choose a language, the website stores that choice (preferred-language, value en or de) in your browser’s local storage. It stores nothing else. The setting is shared with other Devmil Solutions websites on devmil.de, so they open in the same language. This data stays on your device, is never transmitted, and can be deleted in your browser at any time. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in showing the website in the language you chose). Storing the choice on your device does not require consent under § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG, because it is strictly necessary for a service you requested.

6. The app

VaultLama is in early development and has not been released. This section describes the current development builds. It will be updated before the first release and whenever the app’s behaviour changes.

Network

The current builds of VaultLama make no connections to the internet. They show a placeholder screen and contain no vault features. They contain no analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or advertising.

The planned app will connect to the Vaultwarden server whose address you enter. That server is run by you or by someone you choose, not by Devmil Solutions, and its operator processes your account and your encrypted vault under its own terms. Before any feature that contacts a server or another service is released, this section will name what is contacted and which data is sent.

Vault data and credentials

The current builds store no vault data, passwords, keys or tokens. By design, the app’s diagnostic output never contains vault content, credentials, server addresses or file paths.

Permissions

Planned features such as autofill, passkeys and biometric unlock will use permissions and services of your operating system. This policy will describe them before they are released.

7. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16 GDPR), erasure (Art. 17 GDPR), restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR), data portability (Art. 20 GDPR) and objection (Art. 21 GDPR). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, for example the Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, www.lda.bayern.de.

To exercise your rights, contact info [at] devmil [dot] de.

8. Changes

This policy is updated when the website or the app changes. The current version is always available on this page.
